Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Who we are

Vantage Studio ("Vantage," "we," "us") operates vantageweb.site and provides web design and development services, with studio locations in Abuja, Nigeria and Finsbury Park, London, United Kingdom.

Information we collect

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, optional WhatsApp/phone number, and your message.
  • Payment information: when you pay via PayPal, PayPal processes your card details directly — we never receive or store your card number. When you pay by bank transfer, we receive the transaction reference and receipt you send us.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected automatically by our hosting provider.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To deliver, invoice, and support projects you engage us for.
  • To send occasional studio updates if you opt in via the newsletter field — you can unsubscribe at any time.

How your information is processed

Contact form submissions are sent via a serverless function to our email inbox using a third-party transactional email provider. Payments are processed by PayPal or received directly via Moniepoint (Nigeria); we do not store full payment card details on our own systems.

Data retention

We retain enquiry and project correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver services, meet accounting obligations, and resolve any disputes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at accessmakr@gmail.com.

Third parties

We share information only with the service providers needed to operate the site and deliver services — for example, our email delivery provider, PayPal, and our hosting provider — each governed by their own privacy policies.

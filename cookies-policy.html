Cookies Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and, in some cases, remember your preferences or measure usage.

Cookies this site currently uses

  • Strictly necessary: none beyond what your browser stores locally to run the scroll progress bar, FAQ accordions, and seasonal banner — none of these persist after you close the tab or identify you personally.
  • Third-party embeds: the PayPal checkout button and embedded Google Maps on the Contact page may set their own cookies once loaded, governed by PayPal's and Google's respective policies.

If analytics tools (such as Plausible, Google Analytics, or similar) are added in future, this page will be updated to disclose exactly what they collect and how to opt out.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies from PayPal or Google Maps may prevent those specific embeds from working correctly.
